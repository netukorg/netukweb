## When and Where?

* **Where:** [The Brewery](https://www.thebrewery.co.uk/), The Porter Tun (1st Floor), 52 Chiswell Street, LONDON, EC1Y 4SA. [map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/QH62owL3UWJ5o7HA8)

## Accessibility
  
* We want our events to be as inclusive as possible. 
* If there is anything we can do to better your experience with us, please let us know with the accessibility option on the registration form.
* If you'd prefer to check before registering, [Please email us](mailto:team@netuk.org).
* Whether you require a quiet space, assistance with physical accessibility, or support for visual/hearing impairments, we will strive to enhance your event experience to the fullest extent possible.

* The venue is accessible from street level and has lifts to all floors for the rooms we will be using.
  * [Explore the Brewery](https://my.matterport.com/show/?m=8WZ8rvWaLYi)


## Safety and Security

* **Never leave your bag unattended** and report any unattended bags or items in public places.
* There has been increased reports of [drive-by/moped phone thefts](https://www.met.police.uk/cp/crime-prevention/personal-safety-how-to-stay-safe/protect-your-mobile-phone-against-criminals-on-bikes-and-mopeds/). Avoid distractions and **be especially vigilant in busy areas, near roads, or when exiting tube stations or buses**.
* [Staying safe in London](https://www.visitlondon.com/traveller-information/essential-information/staying-safe-in-london).
* Security staff will be present at the venue. Please talk to them if you need help during the event, or go to the registration desk.


## Travel

### Tubes/Railway

* We recommend using the [Citymapper](https://citymapper.com/) app to plan your journey in and around London.
* [thetrainline.com](https://thetrainline.com) for National rail bookings.
* [Transport for London](https://tfl.gov.uk/plan-a-journey/) - Journey planner, bus, tube maps etc.

* If you do not have an Oyster Card, use a Contactless credit or debit card for tube and bus fares. Single fares from ticket machines are expensive!
* Note: Buses in London no longer accept cash payment. Only Oyster or contactless payment is accepted.
* If arriving from outside London, you can buy your national rail ticket including London Underground Zone 1 to get from your arrival station to the tube.

  * BARBICAN STATION (5 minute walk)
  * MOORGATE STATION (5 minute walk)
  * FARRINGDON (10 minute walk)
  * BANK STATION (15 minute walk)
  * LIVERPOOL STREET STATION (15 minute walk)

### Bicycle

* Cycle routes C11/C1. [Route Planner](https://www.cyclestreets.net/)
* Bicycle parking is available at the Brewery. Helmets etc. can be left in the cloakroom.

### By Car

* **We do not recommend driving in central London!** If this is unavoidable:
  * The venue is **inside** [congestion charge zone](https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/driving/congestion-charge/congestion-charge-zone). (Entering the zone 07:00-18:00 Mon-Fri is £15 daily charge).
  * If your vehicle does not meet the [Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) standards, you must also pay the ULEZ charge](https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/driving/ultra-low-emission-zone).

### Parking

* There are no car parking facilities on site at The Brewery. 
  * The nearest public NCP car park can be found at The Barbican. There is also limited pay and display street parking available.
  * Beech Street (8am to Midnight everyday)
  * Finsbury Square (24hrs, Mon-Sat)
  * London Wall (24hrs, Mon-Sat)
  * Aldersgate Street (24hrs, open everyday)

Alternatively, it may be more convenient to stay and park at a hotel further out, but is close to public transport. (e,g, Docklands, Clapham, Vauxhall/Battersea etc.)

### Airports

* [London City Airport](https://www.londoncityairport.com/) ~45mins by DLR/Tube. Convenient for many European cities.
* [London Stansted Airport](https://www.stanstedairport.com/) ~50 mins.
* [London Gatwick Airport](https://www.gatwickairport.com/) ~53mins. Direct train to St Pancras. (Thameslink)
* [London Luton Airport](https://www.gatwickairport.com/) ~23-45mins train from Luton Airport Parkway to London St Pancras (EMR)
* [London Heathrow Airport](https://www.heathrow.com/) ~1h.10mins. by Tube (Piccadilly, Central Line) ~45mins by Elizabeth Line

* London City, Heathrow and Gatwick airports are on the TfL Oyster network for connecting trains. Use Oyster or a contactless payment card at the ticket gates. 

* Luton and Stansted require a separate train ticket.
  * Luton: Buy a ticket to/from **Luton Airport** (NOT Luton Airport Parkway) to include the [DART shuttle](https://www.lutondart.com/) connection from to Luton Airport to Luton Airport Parkway.
  * Stansted: Stansted Express offers the fastest way to travel between London Stansted Airport and London. Trains depart every 30 minutes and take 50 minutes to and from London Liverpool Street.
