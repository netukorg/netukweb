# NetUK2, 7th & 8th July 2025

## When setting our prices, we’ve focused on three main goals:

* Securing sufficient revenue to keep NetUK running
* Ensuring the event is accessible by allowing attendees to self-fund their attendance
* Offering a price below the industry standards set by RIPE and NANOG.
* Offering a price below the industry standards set by RIPE and NANOG.

## Tickets available

* Individual days at £70 each (£58.33 + VAT), or
* Both days at £120 (£100 + VAT)

* A limited number of free tickets are reserved for students, or people who are searching for work in our industry. These need to be approved, and are considered on a first-come, first served basis.

**Ticket sales will close at: 16:00 BST on 26th June.**

* The workshop(s), Newcomer's lunch and Social on Monday 7th July are included at no extra cost (Subject to availability.)
* **Tickets are non-refundable**, but can be transferred until 16:00 on 26th June. (Use the link in your booking confirmation email to change the details.)
* Single day tickets can be moved to another day or upgraded to both days. (Subject to availability.) Please contact us by replying to the confirmation email.
* **Each organisation is limited to four tickets.** If you require more, please contact us.
* This event is open to anyone working in, or seeking work in, related internet technologies.
* As we rely heavily on our sponsors to support the event, those whose primary role involves revenue generation for their employer (e.g. Account Manager, Business Development, Sales Engineer, Solutions Architect) will need to purchase a sponsor ticket. 

* We will collect attendees' Job roles (if applicable) and affiliation.
* Total tickets: 400 (+- 50 or 100)

## Admission

* To speed up registration, please have your ticket ready on arrival either printed or on your phone.
* All tickets/badges will be scanned at the registration desk for **both days**.
* Individual day tickets may only be used on the day they are valid for. (e.g. If you have a ticket for Monday, it will not be valid on Tuesday.) You may be able to change days, subject to availability.
* The organisers reserve the right to refuse admission.
